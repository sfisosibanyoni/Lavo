Welcome to Lavo Wines

Created from love.

Lavo wine originates from the name Lerato, a Southern African name which means love. Lavo Wines is about pleasure, enjoyment, and connecting people around the world — the one thread connecting people of different backgrounds to share congenial association over a glass of wine.

Our wine has been created from love, to be enjoyed with love.

The Farm

De Slange Rivier, Wellington.

Our wines are produced in Wellington, South Africa, at a historic vineyard called De Slange Rivier — founded in 1699 by the French Huguenot Louis Fourie. More than three centuries of vine-growing heritage flows through every vintage we produce.

The Club

Join the wine club.

Receive a monthly wine hamper delivered at your doorstep and enjoy exclusive access to our premium events. LAVO Wine Club members also enjoy tasting previews of all our new wine varieties — the first to taste everything we make.
