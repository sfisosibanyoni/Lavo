Tasting Profile

Notes of pomaceous and tropical fruit like pineapple with a touch of citrus on the nose. Butterscotch and nuttiness on the palate complete this full bodied wine — rich, textured and deeply satisfying.

The Cellar

Winemaking

Machine harvested grapes were pressed and cleared using flotation — a gentle technique that preserves the delicate fruit aromas and natural freshness of the variety.

All work was done as reductively as possible to prevent oxidation. The juice was racked with a little of the fermentation lees to a fermentation tank, where fermentation took place at a cool 16°C.

After fermentation, the wine was pumped off the thick lees and stored on the thin lees, where it developed texture and the characteristic butterscotch richness that defines this wine.
