Member Benefit

Discounts on Purchases

As a Lavo Wine Club member, every bottle you purchase, whether inside or outside your monthly delivery, comes with an exclusive member discount. Connoisseur members enjoy 10% off all purchases. Exclusive members receive 15% off. Your love of wine should never cost more than it has to.

Member Benefit

Member Exclusive Events

Member Wine Club Days are reserved exclusively for our club members. These are intimate gatherings at the estate where you get to meet the winemaker, explore the production process, and taste wines before they are available to the public. Exclusive tier members receive priority invitations and reserved seating.

Member Benefit

Latest Wine Releases

Wine Club members are the first to know, and the first to taste. When Lavo releases a new vintage or a new varietal, members receive advance notice and exclusive first-order allocation before wines are made available to the general public. Some releases are available to members only.

Wine Club

How it works

Joining the Lavo Wine Club is simple. We handle the curation, the packaging, and the delivery. All you need to do is choose your tier and enjoy the wine.

01

Choose your Tier

Select the Connoisseur or Exclusive membership tier based on the number of bottles and benefits that best suit your lifestyle.

02

Complete your Profile

Fill in your delivery details and wine preferences so we can personalise your monthly selection to your palate.

03

Receive your Wines

Your curated Lavo selection is delivered to your door each month in our signature packaging, ready to open, pour, and enjoy.

04

Discover & Share

Read your monthly tasting notes, explore pairing suggestions, and stay connected to exclusive events, new releases, and member-only experiences.

01 Curated Selection

Every bottle in your monthly delivery is handpicked from the Lavo range: a deliberate combination of varietals chosen to complement the season, your palate, and the art of great winemaking.

02 Always a Member

No lock-in. No conditions. Your membership rolls month to month and you remain in full control. Pause, skip, or cancel at any time. Your benefits stay active for your current billing period.

03 Dedicated Service

Our team is on hand for every query: deliveries, preferences, recommendations. We are committed to making your membership effortless. Reach us directly at info@lavowineandfood.com.
